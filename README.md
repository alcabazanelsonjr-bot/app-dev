# Favorite Series

## Title: Loki
**Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Sci-Fi  
**Studio:** Marvel Studios  
**Platform:** Disney+

### 🌀 About the Series
*Loki* follows the God of Mischief after the events of **Avengers: Endgame**, where he escapes with the Tesseract and gets captured by the **Time Variance Authority (TVA)**.  
The series explores **time travel, alternate timelines, and Loki’s journey of self-discovery**.
